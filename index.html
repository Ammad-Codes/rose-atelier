Our Story

Baked with love, finished by hand

Born in a small kitchen with one oven and a big dream, Rosé Atelier began as birthday cakes for friends and family. Today every cake is still baked in small batches, decorated by hand and delivered with care.

We use real butter, Belgian chocolate, fresh fruit and rose water, with no shortcuts. Eggless and sugar-reduced options are always available on request.
